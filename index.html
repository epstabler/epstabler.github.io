I'm an Emeritus Professor in the Department of Linguistics at UCLA

I study mathematical models of language, parsing, and language learning, with a focus on syntax and semantics.

I am also interested in the philosophy of logic, language, and mind, which is what I studied in graduate school and much of what I think about in my old age.

I received my Ph.D. from MIT in 1981, advised by Jerry Fodor.
